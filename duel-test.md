# Duel test — Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off

Method: proxy judging by hand, applying the kit judge brief verbatim
(criteria in order: 1. names an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy
AI agents; 2. absurd specificity; 3. business sense — plausible buyer,
plausible revenue, reason to exist; 4. lazy-agent fit; 5. crisp and funny).
Every game = two calls, one in each order; a call won at margin m scores
0.5 + m/10 for the winner; a game's score is the mean of its two calls.
No OpenRouter key is available on this VM, so no live judge calls were made.

Fixed house-style anchors (invented proxies — the kit seals the real
anchors/rungs, so these stand in for weak → strong):
- W (weak): "A startup that helps AI agents do tasks faster and better with smart automation tools for everyone."
- M (medium): "A $5-a-month service that writes meeting notes for lazy AI agents so their owners think they attended the call."
- S (strong): "A $3-a-month service that files your agent's expense reports, turns idle GPU time into billable 'research,' and emails finance a PDF nobody reads."

## Winner

**#11 (191 chars / 191 bytes):**
"A $4-a-month nanny that watches your agent's 48-hour jobs, texts the human 'still working, looking great' every six hours, and never once checks the logs, for total peace of mind, guaranteed."

Why it wins: the most absurdly specific moment (a nanny for 48-hour jobs,
texting the human every six hours), a plausible buyer and price ($4/month
from the agent's owner), the laziest possible agent fit (it never checks
the logs), and the joke lands in one read. Combined mean 0.687, above the
0.60 target.
